Dark Store Inventory Intelligence System

Python Pandas NumPy XGBoost Scikit-learn Streamlit

Built a machine learning application to predict SKU-level stockout risks within the next 48 hours for a rapid grocery delivery (dark store) operation. Worked with over 80,700 records and engineered more than 30 features to train an XGBoost classification model.

The AI Layoff Myth – What the Data Actually Says

MySQL SQL Power BI Data Analysis

Analysed 4,337 layoff events from 2020 to 2026 to investigate whether AI adoption is truly driving workforce reductions. Used MySQL for data staging, cleaning, deduplication and analysis, then created Power BI visualizations to explore layoff periods, company trends and AI-related workforce changes.

Key finding: 52% of major 2026 layoffs cited AI as a factor — yet most of those same companies had simultaneously increased their overall headcount, suggesting restructuring rather than reduction.
