About Me

I'm Syed Rehan, an aspiring Data Analyst with a background in Civil Engineering. After completing my B.E. and M.Tech in Structural Engineering, I worked as a Civil Engineer at Rahul Bora Engineers & Associates, where I used Excel automation and structured reporting to manage calculations and documentation for more than 40 structural members.

That hands-on experience with data and patterns sparked a genuine interest in analytics. I went on to complete a Postgraduate Program in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence at Imarticus Learning, building skills in Python, SQL, statistics, machine learning and data visualization. I now focus on practical projects that turn raw data into clear, actionable insights.
